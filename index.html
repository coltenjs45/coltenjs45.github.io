Where I'm headed

I grew up as the older brother, and looking out for people has always been part of who I am. Cybersecurity is the professional version of that. My grandmother has had her card and personal information stolen more than once, and seeing what that did to her made it clear that security isn't only about corporations and governments. It's about ordinary people and their families.

When I graduate in May 2027, I want a full-time role in security engineering, risk and compliance, or security auditing, and I want to keep adding certifications on top of the CompTIA Security+ I earned in 2026. Longer term, I want to be the engineer who can both secure the network and explain to a non-technical coworker why it matters. My internship showed me that the second skill is just as important as the first.

I grew up wrestling, and I approach security the same way: it's a contest against an opponent who keeps adapting, and the only way to stay ahead is steady, consistent work.
