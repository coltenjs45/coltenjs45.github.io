Soft skills

I came in confident in my technical skills from lab work at Iowa State, but my biggest challenge was explaining why a security practice mattered to someone without a security vocabulary. I picked up an approach from a help desk coworker who explained mapped network drives as a filing cabinet: only people with the right key can open certain drawers, and the shared cabinet keeps a safe copy if your own gets damaged. I started using analogies like that myself, and my conversations with coworkers about security got noticeably smoother.
